# Aruna Shopping List

Aruna Shopping List keeps a shopping list that you fill by talking to Aruna, Hariku's command
bar. It is an example from the Hariku Developer SDK, and needs Hariku 2.9 or later.

## Adding something

Open Aruna with Ctrl+Alt+Backspace, then type or say one of these, with what you need in the
middle:

- "add milk to my shopping list"
- "add bread to the shopping list"
- "put eggs on my shopping list"
- "tambahkan susu ke daftar belanja"

Aruna asks "Add "milk" to your shopping list?". Press Enter or answer "yes" to add it, or
press Escape or answer "no" to leave it off.

## Hearing the list

Tell Aruna "shopping list" or "what do I need to buy". Aruna reads the list. While "Keep Aruna
open after an answer" is on in Preferences, Aruna (it is at first), Aruna stays open, so you can
add something straight away.

## Emptying the list

Tell Aruna "clear my shopping list". Aruna closes and a window asks whether to remove
everything: choose Yes or No.

## Keys and commands

- "Read my shopping list": no key
- "Clear my shopping list": no key

Give them a key in Preferences, Input Gestures, under Aruna Shopping List.

## Privacy

The list is saved only on your computer, in Hariku's data folder. Nothing is sent anywhere.
