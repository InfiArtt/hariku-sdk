# Proverbs

Proverbs says an old proverb when you ask, in English or in Indonesian, whichever Hariku speaks.
It is an example from the Hariku Developer SDK, made to show how an extension ships its own
guide: this one. It needs Hariku 2.11 or later.

## Hearing a proverb

Tell Aruna "say a proverb": press Ctrl+Alt+Backspace, then type or say it. Each time you get
one of five old proverbs, picked at random.

## Opening this guide

- Help, Extension guides..., then choose Proverbs.
- The Guide button in the Extension Manager, on the Installed tab.
- Tell Aruna "guide for proverbs" or "panduan peribahasa".
- Tell Aruna "open the proverbs guide".

The guide opens in your web browser. With NVDA, press H to go from heading to heading.

## Keys and commands

- "Say a proverb": no key
- "Open the Proverbs guide": no key

Give them a key in Preferences, Input Gestures, under Proverbs.

## Privacy

Proverbs sends nothing anywhere and saves nothing.
