# Daily Goal

Daily Goal keeps your goal for the day and says it when you ask. It is an example from the
Hariku Developer SDK.

## Writing your goal

Open Preferences, Daily Goal, and type your goal in "Your goal for today:". Press OK to save it.

To go straight to that page, tell Aruna "open the daily goal settings".

## Hearing your goal

Press Shift+Y while Hariku's window has the focus, or tell Aruna "my goal for today".

## Settings

In Preferences, Daily Goal:

- "Your goal for today:" is what Daily Goal says, up to 200 characters.
- "When other speech is still going:" chooses "Wait for it to finish", or "Interrupt it" to hear
  your goal at once.
- "Say the date first" starts with today's date.

## Keys and commands

- Shift+Y: "Say my goal for today"
- No key: "Open the Daily Goal settings"

Give them other keys in Preferences, Input Gestures, under Daily Goal.

## Privacy

Your goal is saved only on your computer, in Hariku's data folder. Nothing is sent anywhere.
