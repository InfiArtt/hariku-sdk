# Hello Hotkey

Hello Hotkey greets you when you press a key. It is an example from the Hariku Developer SDK.

## Getting a greeting

Press Shift+H while Hariku's window has the focus. Hello Hotkey says hello.

Press Shift+H twice quickly, and it tells you the time as well.

You can also ask Aruna: press Ctrl+Alt+Backspace, then type or say "greet me".

## Keys and commands

- Shift+H: "Greet me" (twice quickly: the time too)

Give it another key, or make it work while you are in another program, in Preferences, Input
Gestures, under Hello Hotkey.

## Privacy

Hello Hotkey sends nothing anywhere and saves nothing.
